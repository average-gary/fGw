# Planting Station

A planting station is a single point of soil prepared to grow one plant
(or one tight cluster of plants). FGW does not amend whole beds; it
amends stations. The undisturbed soil between stations is structure;
the stations are kitchens.

## Spacing

- **In-row spacing**: {{in_row_cm}} cm between stations along a row.
- **Row spacing**: {{row_cm}} cm between rows.

That gives roughly 22 stations per square metre at the densest end of
the spectrum. For larger crops (squash, brassicas) you double or triple
the in-row spacing but keep the row spacing.

The 60 × 75 cm grid is also walkable: you can step between rows without
crushing canopy, and you can kneel between stations to weed by hand.

## Depth — Organic Input

Dig the station {{organic_input_depth_cm}} cm deep. Mix in compost,
worm castings, or finished pile material at this full depth. The
{{organic_input_depth_cm}} cm number matters: shallower starves the
roots in week 4; deeper buries the active biology below where roots
will reach.

## Depth — Inorganic Input (Optional)

For mineral-deficient soils, add inorganic amendments
({{inorganic_input_depth_cm}} cm into the bottom of the station).
Examples: rock dust, gypsum, biochar. These move slowly through soil,
so placing them deep is correct.

## How To Build One

1. Mark the station with a stake or a finger-pressed dimple.
2. Dig a {{organic_input_depth_cm}} cm-deep hole, about a hand's-width
   wide.
3. Pile the excavated topsoil to the side.
4. (Optional) Sprinkle inorganic amendment in the bottom
   {{inorganic_input_depth_cm}} cm.
5. Mix compost into the excavated topsoil at roughly 1:1 by volume.
6. Backfill the hole with the mixed soil, mounded slightly.
7. Plant. Water in. Mulch with God's Blanket.

## Why Stations And Not Beds

Beds spread amendment thin. Stations concentrate it where the plant
will actually use it. A 2 m row with 4 stations gets the same compost
as a 2 m row of bed-amended soil — and grows plants twice as vigorously
because the compost is concentrated under each root system.
