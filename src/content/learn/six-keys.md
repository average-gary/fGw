# The Six Management Keys

Freedom Garden Way (FGW) builds soil and crops by attending to six
inseparable practices. Skip any one and the whole system loses its rhythm.
Use them together — they reinforce each other.

## 1. On Time

Do every task on the day the field guide calls for it. Compost piles get
turned on day 3, 6, 9, 19, 29, 39 — not "around then." Mulch goes back
down before the soil is exposed for a single afternoon. Garden time is
calendar time.

## 2. On Spec

Build to the specified shape and depth. A 2 m × 2 m × 2 m pile, layered
in the prescribed thicknesses. A planting station with 60 cm in-row and
75 cm row spacing, 15 cm of organic input. Out-of-spec gives unreliable
results.

## 3. Full Inputs

Use the full ingredient set every time: manure, green, woody, dry,
water. Compost is a recipe, not a heap of yard waste. Same goes for
planting stations — full organic depth, full mulch coverage.

## 4. Plant On Time

Plants want to be in the ground when the calendar says, not when it is
convenient. Late starts compound: late seedlings → late harvest → late
seed save → late next year.

## 5. Cover the Soil

Bare soil is bleeding soil. God's Blanket — a 2.5 cm coat of mulch — goes
over 100% of the cropped area, all year. Living roots cover what mulch
cannot.

## 6. Save Seeds

Every season, save seeds from the strongest plants for next year. Local
adaptation compounds. A chapter that has saved seed for five years has
varieties no catalog will ever sell you.
