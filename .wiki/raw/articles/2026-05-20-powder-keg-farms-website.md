# Powder Keg Farms — chapter context

Primary chapter for the fGw app. The user (Gary Krause) and Gini LaMaster are the named contacts.

## Confirmed facts

- **Location**: 1968 Powder Keg Lane, High View, WV 26808.
- **Climate**: USDA Zone 6b; humid-continental; snowy winter, hot humid summer; last-frost typically late April / early May.
- **Identity**: "the first and only model demonstration farm in the country for Farming God's Way" (self-claim, January 2024).
- **Partner**: Eukarya Christian Academy (CSA pickup site; teaching FGW to students/faculty).
