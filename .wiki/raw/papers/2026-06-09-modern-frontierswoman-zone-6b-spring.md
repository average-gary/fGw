# Modern Frontierswoman — Zone 6B Spring Planting Guide

## Why ingested

The Happy Acres "last week of April" 6b figure is too aggressive for high-elevation WV; this source's "May 10–15" 6b last-frost is the conservative bound the chapter article needs for mountain microclimates.

## Key findings

- Zone 6B last frost given as **May 10–15**.
- **Corn**: May 15 – June 5; depth 1–1.5 in; 8–12 in spacing in blocks.
- **Green beans**: May 15 – June 5; 1 in deep; 3–4 in spacing.
- **Squash**: May 20 – June 5.
- "1-week buffer for sensitive crops; check 10-day forecast" guidance.

## Notes for FGW calendar translation

- Use as the upper-elevation bound; mountain-county WV chapter members should plant by these dates rather than the lowland Happy Acres / WVU dates.
