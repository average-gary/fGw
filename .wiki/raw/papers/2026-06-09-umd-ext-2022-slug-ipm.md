# UMD 2022 Slug IPM

## Why ingested

Most recent (2022) Mid-Atlantic synthesis. Names "poorly drained" + "high residue" + "wet spring" as the failure-mode triple — which exactly describes WV bottomland under FGW's prescription in a Zone 6b spring.

## Key findings

- "Slug populations are more likely to be damaging in **poorly drained fields with a lot of surface residue and little to no soil disturbance**."
- "Slugs tend to cause problems in **low-disturbance fields with lots of residue, especially in wet springs following mild winters**."
- BMP: row cleaners removing **10–12 in wide residue strips** — partial bare soil for the seed row, directly contradicting FGW's 100% mulch mandate.

## Notes for FGW calendar translation

- The "poorly drained" qualifier is important: WV upland sites with good drainage are less exposed to this failure mode than WV bottomland — the chapter article should make site selection explicit.
- The 10–12 in row-strip BMP is a direct precedent for the wiki's preferred [delay-the-blanket adaptation](../../wiki/concepts/mulch-spring-warming-penalty.md): wait to apply the full mulch until after emergence.
