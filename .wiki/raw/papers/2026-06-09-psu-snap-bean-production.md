# Snap Bean Production (Penn State Extension)

## Why ingested

Cross-state operational calendar from a directly-adjacent extension service. PSU's 55°F soil-temp gate is **5°F lower than WVU's 60°F** — a useful spread to flag for the chapter article (different cultivars / measurement protocols / risk tolerances).

## Key findings

- **Soil temp ≥ 55°F** at planting; **air ≥ 60°F**.
- **Successive plantings every 2 weeks through August**.
- **Seeding depth**: 0.5–1.0 in (1.5 in if dry).
- **pH**: 5.8–6.6.
- **PA harvest window**: June – October (cultivar 46–60 days).
- **Thermal envelope**: growth stalls below 50°F or above 80°F — relevant to summer pod set in hot WV lowlands.

## Notes for FGW calendar translation

- The chapter article should report the WVU 60°F figure as the conservative gate (the wiki's policy is to defer to the home-state extension when it's stricter than neighbors).
- The "every 2 weeks through August" succession statement is what gives the FGW posters' "row 1 / row 2 / row 3" sequencing a defensible Zone 6b cadence.
- The 80°F-stall ceiling matters: a WV mid-July planting that hits a hot first 10 days germinates poorly, irrespective of mulch — relevant for whether the chapter article warns against the late part of the planting window in hot summers.
