# Happy Acres Zone 6b Schedule

## Why ingested

True Zone 6b (southern Indiana) practitioner — climate close enough to WV 6b that the dates are usable as-is for chapter members in lowland WV. Bracketing this against Pam Dawling's 7a schedule gives the chapter article a defensible north / south corridor for WV.

## Key findings

- Zone 6b southern Indiana: ~180 frost-free days; **last frost last week of April; first frost last week of October**.
- **Snap beans** direct-sow mid–late April; **corn + green bean + cucurbits** early May.
- Soil-temp gates: ≥ 60°F corn / beet, ≥ 65°F bean / pepper / squash.
- **Late July / early August second sowing** of snap beans, beets, carrots, chard, peas, turnip.
- **Pole-bean varieties**: Cherokee Trail of Tears + Good Mother Stallard (both dry beans), direct-sown in May after soil > 65°F.
- **Greasy beans grown** as Appalachian heirlooms in 6b (Bertie Best, Lazy Wife, Robe Mountain, Turkey Craw).

## Notes for FGW calendar translation

- "Last week of April" last-frost matches the 2023 USDA shift for low-elevation 6b but is **earlier than what most pre-2023 WV extension publications assume** — the chapter article should note this.
- Cherokee Trail of Tears pole bean is a cultural-heritage cultivar that FGW posters' generic "beans" line can be filled with for chapter use; explicit variety naming makes the article actionable.
