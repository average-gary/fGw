# Nitrogen Fertilization of Corn / Pre-sidedress Soil Nitrate Test

## Why ingested

The most rigorous regional reference for **when in the calendar** to top-dress corn in Zone 6a–6b. FGW's Master Sequence Posters specify "top-dressing" without tying it to a growth stage; this source provides the V4–V6 anchor used by extension agronomists across the Mid-Atlantic.

## Key findings

- **Sidedress N window**: corn 10–20 inches tall — V4 to V6.
- **PSNT sampling**: at 12 inches tall, **at least one week before sidedress** so the result drives the rate.
- **Split**: 50–90% of total N applied at sidedress (the rest at planting); rationale is corn N uptake is minimal until ~35 days after emergence, then a sharp uptake spurt.
- **N rates**: 100–220 lb N/acre grain; 120–240 lb N/acre silage by yield goal.
- Avoiding pre-plant excess prevents leaching during cold-spring rainfall events typical of Zone 6b.

## Notes for FGW calendar translation

- For an early-May plant date in WV 6b, V4 ≈ Memorial Day, V6 ≈ second week of June. That's the FGW "top-dressing" window translated into chapter-calendar terms.
- The "minimal uptake until day ~35" finding lines up with FGW's principle of applying compost early but additional nitrogen later — the wiki's chapter article should make the timing rationale explicit.
- PSNT is optional for FGW practitioners; the tools FGW assumes (well-built compost) deliver N in a different release pattern, but the V4–V6 timing window remains the right calendar anchor for any added top-dress.
