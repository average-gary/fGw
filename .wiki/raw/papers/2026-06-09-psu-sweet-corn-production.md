# Sweet Corn Production (Penn State Extension)

## Why ingested

PSU is the closest north-of-WV authority and brackets WV's elevation/climate range cleanly: northern PA conditions match WV's mountain counties; southern PA matches WV's lowlands and panhandle. The "north WV plants like northern PA, south WV plants like southern PA" rule of thumb is built on this source.

## Key findings

- **Southern PA**: plant early-to-mid April.
- **Northern PA**: plant mid-to-late May.
- **Soil temp**: ≥ 55°F for standard varieties (matches WVU; tighter than VCE's 65°F sh2 figure).
- **Side-dress N**: at 24 in plant height (cross-validates PSU PSNT V4–V6 / 10–20 in window — sweet corn is sidedressed slightly later than field corn in this bulletin).
- **PA harvest window**: early July through end of September.
- **pH**: 5.8–6.6.
- **Banded fertilizer preferred** over broadcast.

## Notes for FGW calendar translation

- The north-PA / south-PA bracket gives the chapter article a credible way to handle WV's elevation range without inventing dates: panhandle and Greenbrier Valley take the southern-PA dates; Pocahontas/Tucker/Randolph counties take the northern-PA dates.
- The 24-in sidedress height differs slightly from the PSNT V4–V6 figure — PSU is using a sweet-corn-specific later-stage rule because sweet corn is harvested at milk stage, not black layer; the wiki article should keep both anchors and note when to use each.
