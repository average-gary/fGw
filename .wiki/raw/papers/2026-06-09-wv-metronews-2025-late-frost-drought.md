# Leonhardt — Drought Recovery + Late Frost (2025)

## Why ingested

A 2025 WV-specific reality check on the "shift planting earlier" trend — even with warmer 30-year averages, the false-spring tail still bites. The chapter article cites this to keep frost-prudent language.

## Key findings

- **Late spring frost (2025)** reduced fruit crops and damaged vegetable plantings statewide.
- Statewide **~40% honeybee hive loss** attributed to drought-driven pollen scarcity (compounding pollination risk for FGW practitioners growing legumes / cucurbits).
- Aquifer / soil moisture deficits persisted into June 2025 despite improved rainfall numbers.

## Notes for FGW calendar translation

- The chapter article should keep "median last frost" plus a "10% probability last frost" date for each station — the 50% line is what most planting recommendations target, but the 10% line is what conservative growers (especially first-year FGW practitioners with no spare seed) should default to.
- Honeybee loss adds risk to bean / squash flowering dates; encourage a wider spatial planting strategy + staggered succession to spread pollination risk.
