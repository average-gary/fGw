# 40 CFR §503 Appendix B — Pathogen Reduction Methods (PFRP)

The U.S. federal regulatory floor for "pathogen-reduced" compost. Published in 1993 under the Clean Water Act §405; still the operative standard.

## The thresholds

### (a) Windrow composting
> The temperature of the compost pile shall be maintained at 55 °C or higher for **15 days or longer**. During the period when the compost is maintained at 55 °C or higher, there shall be **a minimum of five turnings of the windrow**.

### (b) Aerated static pile
> The temperature of the compost pile shall be maintained at 55 °C or higher for **three days**.

### (c) Within-vessel
> The temperature of the compost pile shall be maintained at 55 °C or higher for **three days**.

## Why ingested

- It is the single legally-meaningful "compost is sanitized" line in the US.
- Powder Keg's CSA distribution may or may not cross into regulated commerce depending on WV state rules — but PFRP is the floor anyone wanting to *sell* compost in the US must clear.
- Lets the wiki produce the missing comparison: **does FGW's 6-turn schedule at days 3/6/9/19/29/39 satisfy PFRP windrow?**

## FGW vs PFRP — comparison

| Requirement | PFRP windrow | FGW Field Guide |
|---|---|---|
| Sustained temperature | ≥55 °C for ≥15 days | Target 55–68 °C; Field Guide says decomposition continues "for 2 months" through 5–6 turns |
| Number of turnings during high-temp period | ≥5 | 5–6 turns at days 3/6/9/19/29/39 |
| Turn-window duration | implicit (must overlap the 15+ days) | 36 days (day 3 to day 39) |
| Temperature instrumentation | implicit calibrated thermometer (TMECC method 03.05) | "8 mm steel rod inserted for 5 sec — if you can hold for 5 sec it's <68 °C" |

**Verdict (working draft for `wiki/theses/`)**: FGW *prescribes* a regimen that, if observed and instrumented properly, **meets PFRP windrow** (6 turns ≥ 5; 36-day high-temp window ≥ 15 days). However, FGW's hand-feel temperature heuristic is not TMECC-defensible — a chapter pile is **regimen-compliant** but not **measurement-compliant**. To claim PFRP, the chapter would need a calibrated probe and a temperature log.
