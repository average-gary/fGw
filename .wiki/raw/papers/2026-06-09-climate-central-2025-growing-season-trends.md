# Climate Central 2025 — Growing Season Trends

## Why ingested

Provides regional frost-free-day numbers that bracket WV (Ohio Valley + Northeast) — translates directly into "shift planting windows roughly 2 weeks earlier than 1990s tables suggest" guidance for the chapter article.

## Key findings

- Analyzed 198 U.S. cities, 1970–2024.
- 87% (172 cities) show a longer freeze-free season; average increase ≈ **20 frost-free days** since 1970.
- **Ohio Valley region: +13 days** (188-day average freeze-free season).
- **Northeast region: +17 days** (180-day average freeze-free season).
- Methodology: peer-reviewed Climate Central methodology.

## Notes for FGW calendar translation

- WV straddles Ohio Valley (western, southern) and Northeast (eastern panhandle, mountains) — the chapter article should use 188 d (lowlands) and 180 d (mountains) as defensible ranges.
- Combined with the 2023 USDA hardiness-zone shift, this is the second numerical reason older WV planting tables are mid-week-or-more conservative against current conditions.
- Counter-evidence: the [WV MetroNews 2025 late-frost report](2026-06-09-wv-metronews-2025-late-frost-drought.md) shows that even with longer averages, false-spring damage still happens — the chapter article must keep frost-prudent risk language even as it shifts dates earlier.
