# USDA 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map Update

## Why ingested

Authoritative reference for the 2023 zone-map shift used by the wiki to anchor the Zone 6b WV translation in 2026 conditions, not 1990s climatology.

## Key findings

- First update since 2012; based on 30-year averages (1991–2020) of lowest annual winter temperatures.
- Built on 13,412 weather stations (vs 7,983 in 2012) — substantially better resolution.
- About half the country shifted to the next warmer half-zone; the other half held.
- WV in the 2023 map: most of the state is **6a–6b** with the eastern panhandle and southern lowlands trending **7a**; the highest elevations remain 5b.
- Interactive GIS format with expanded "Tips for Growers" section.

## Notes for FGW calendar translation

- Many older WV extension calendars cite a pre-2023 zone — the chapter article should default to the 2023 map and flag the older-source caveat where it cites a 2012-or-earlier extension publication.
- The minimum-temperature definition the map encodes is for **winter cold tolerance**, not last-frost or growing-degree-day timing — those still need NCEI normals (1991–2020) and station-by-station NWS frost-freeze probabilities. The map alone is necessary but not sufficient for a calendar.
