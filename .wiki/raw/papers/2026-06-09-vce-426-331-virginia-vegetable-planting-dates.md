# Virginia's Home Garden Vegetable Planting Guide (VCE 426-331 / SPES-673P)

VCE-published, peer-reviewed extension guide with planting windows tabulated for each USDA hardiness zone present in Virginia and the surrounding Mid-Atlantic. Authored by Jadrnicek (organic-systems heritage, formerly Clemson) and Hessler (Director, Homefield Farm at Virginia Tech).

## Why ingested

- The only authoritative dated table found that organizes maize and bean planting windows by hardiness zone *6a / 6b / 7a / 7b*. WV spans 5b–7a (per USDA 2023 update); the table covers the dominant chapter range directly.
- VA shares climate, latitude, and elevation banding with WV; Greenbrier Valley and the WV eastern panhandle behave climatologically like adjacent VA counties.
- Will serve as the spine of `wiki/concepts/fgw-calendar-zone-6b-translation.md` — the chapter-side companion to the FGW Master Sequence Posters.

## Key calendar findings (verbatim windows)

| Crop | Zone 6a | Zone 6b | Zone 7a |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sweet corn (spring) | May 5 – Jul 10 | Apr 20 – Jul 10 | Apr 10 – Jul 10 |
| Snap bean (spring) | May 15 – Jun 15 | May 5 – Jun 25 | Apr 25 – Jul 5 |
| Snap bean (fall) | Jun 15 – Jul 15 | Jun 25 – Jul 25 | Jul 1 – Aug 5 |
| Pole bean (spring) | May 25 – Jun 25 | May 15 – Jun 15 | May 5 – Jun 5 |
| Pole bean (fall) | Jun 25 – Jul 5 | Jun 15 – Jul 1 | Jun 5 – Jun 25 |

## Notes for translation work

- "Spring" sweet corn opens roughly **two weeks earlier** in Zone 6b vs Zone 6a — consistent with the +0.5-zone shift on the 2023 USDA map.
- Bean fall-window dates are tight: in 6b a snap bean planted as late as Jul 25 is on a 60-day clock against ~Oct 15 first frost in 6b lowlands; pole beans drop out earlier.
- Authors do not recommend a fall sweet-corn planting in Zone 6b — silking date pushes too close to first frost.
