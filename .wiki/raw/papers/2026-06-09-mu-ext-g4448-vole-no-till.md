# MU Extension G4448 — Voles in No-Till

## Why ingested

The hardest-documented conflict between the FGW prescription and a Mid-Atlantic / Lower-Midwest pest reality. The 80–100% stand-loss figure is decisive.

## Key findings

- "Plant stand reductions may reach **80 to 100 percent** in fields with dense vole populations."
- Damage window: **first 21 to 28 days after planting** — exactly the WV April–May seedling window.
- Voles "require a full canopy cover for protection from predators" — i.e., FGW's "God's blanket" mulch IS vole habitat.
- Effective control = "Destroying the vole colony, cover and food supply by **clean tilling or plowing before planting**" — direct contradiction of FGW's no-plough rule.

## Notes for FGW calendar translation

- The chapter article must mention vole pressure as an *independent* failure mode beyond slugs: voles attack established seedlings, slugs attack germinating seed; both load the cold-wet-mulched-seedbed risk model.
- The 21–28 day damage window is the same window the wiki's [mulch-spring-warming-penalty](../../wiki/concepts/mulch-spring-warming-penalty.md) flags for delayed emergence — the two failure modes compound.
- Mitigation menu (compatible with FGW): perimeter mowing, raptor perches, snake habitat, cat / dog presence — none require tillage. The chapter article should suggest these as no-tillage-compatible options.
