# Growing Sweet Corn in West Virginia

WVU Extension state-of-record source for sweet corn culture in WV. Directly applicable for both market gardeners and the FGW chapter audience.

## Why ingested

WV-resident authority. Necessary citation for any WV-specific calendar claim made in `wiki/concepts/fgw-calendar-zone-6b-translation.md` — the wiki cites home-state extension whenever it exists.

## Key findings

- **Planting window**: late April → mid-July; earliest plantings mid–late April if soil temperature and weather permit.
- **Soil temperature thresholds (4-inch depth)**:
  - Standard sugary (su): 55°F
  - Sugar-enhanced (se): 60°F
  - Supersweet (sh2): 60°F
- **Days to maturity**: 63–100 by variety. Examples: Bodacious 75, Honey & Pearl 78, How Sweet It Is 80.
- **Succession**: every 2 weeks for continuous summer supply.
- **Spacing**: 9–12 in within row, 30–36 in between rows.
- **Seeding depth**: 0.5 in cool/moist soil; 1–1.5 in warm/dry soil.
- **Sidedress N**: half pre-plant + half "when four to five leaves are fully expanded" — V4–V5 calendar anchor for FGW's top-dressing operation.
- **Harvest**: ≈ 20 days after first silk.

## Notes for FGW calendar translation

- WVU's "mid-late April" plant date sits roughly between VCE's Zone 6a (May 5) and Zone 6b (Apr 20) recommendations — consistent with most WV being 6b lowland / 6a hilltop.
- The 55°F-soil gate for standard sweet corn is a **cleaner FGW calendar trigger** than calendar-date alone, since it bakes in elevation, slope, and year-to-year variability.
- Sidedress at V4–V5 is the operation FGW's posters call "top-dressing"; in WV it falls roughly Memorial Day → mid-June for an early-May plant date.
