# Twin Oaks Garden Task List for May (Pam Dawling)

## Why ingested

The closest published practitioner with weekly-resolution dates the wiki could find. Twin Oaks is **central VA, Zone 7a, last frost ~Apr 20** — about 2 weeks earlier than Zone 6b WV, so dates translate by adding ~14 days for the chapter article.

## Key findings

- **Corn #1** already planted by early May; **corn #2** sown late May with second tilling/undersowing of soy when corn is 12 in tall (~30 days after sowing).
- **Beans #2: 5/14** (28 days after #1).
- **Limas #1**: early May.
- **Cowpeas #1**: early May (68-day variety); **cowpeas #2**: late May.
- Soil-temp + phenology triggers (e.g., dogwood petal-drop = 70°F soil for peppers/melons).
- May harvests start: asparagus, peas, strawberries, garlic scapes.

## Notes for FGW calendar translation

- The "tilling at corn 12 in tall + undersow" rule is similar to FGW's between-row weed management on the Master Sequence Posters (zero-tillage + targeted weeding) — the chapter article should compare and note where they diverge.
- Phenology cues (dogwood petal-drop) are useful for FGW members who don't have a soil thermometer — a fall-back the chapter article can include without inventing data.
