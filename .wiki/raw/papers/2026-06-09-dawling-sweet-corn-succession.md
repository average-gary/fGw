# Sweet Corn Succession (Pam Dawling)

## Why ingested

Provides a practitioner-tested six-planting succession cadence with explicit dates, soil-temp/days-to-germination data, and phenology cues — exactly the sequence FGW's Master Sequence Posters anchor on, but for a humid-temperate climate.

## Key findings

- Twin Oaks succession-corn calendar (Zone 7a): **4/26, 5/19, 6/6, 6/24, 7/7, 7/16** — gaps 23/18/18/13/9 days (closing as season warms).
- **Soil-temp germination table**:
  - 50°F → 22 days
  - 59°F → 12 days
  - 68°F → 7 days
  - 77°F → 4 days
- **Cultivation cadence**: hoeing at 7, 14, 21 days (corn 6–12 in), 35 days (corn 18–20 in); undersow cover at 28–35 days after emergence.
- **Phenology cue**: "oak leaves as big as squirrel's ears" → soil ready.
- **Last-sowing math**: days-to-maturity + 7–14 day harvest window subtracted from first frost.

## Notes for FGW calendar translation

- Translated to Zone 6b WV (+~14 days): 5/10, 6/2, 6/20, 7/8, 7/21, 7/30. The last two plantings get tight against first frost in mountain WV; flag in the chapter article.
- The germination-table is the single best argument the chapter article has for "wait for soil to warm" over "plant by date" — it is a 5× difference in days-to-germination across the realistic spring soil-temp range.
- The closing succession gap (23 → 9 days) reflects the heat-driven acceleration of the corn growth curve — relevant for FGW posters' implicit assumption of constant-cadence sequencing.
