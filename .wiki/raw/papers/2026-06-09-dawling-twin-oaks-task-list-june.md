# Twin Oaks Garden Task List for June (Pam Dawling)

## Why ingested

Carries the May tasklist forward into the bean and dry-bean window — Zone 7a Virginia practitioner data the chapter article translates +2 weeks for WV Zone 6b.

## Key findings

- **Corn #3, edamame #3, beans #3** sown early June.
- **Corn #4, drying beans, limas #2** sown mid-June.
- **Drying beans + beans #4 + edamame #4** sown 6/23–6/25 (so dry beans go in late June for Aug/Sep harvest in 7a).
- **Bulb onion harvest** 6/11–6/30; garlic curing protocol (60–70°F, never 40–50°F).
- **Sweet-corn cultivation rule**: till twice; undersow at 2nd tilling (~30 days) when 12 in high; thin to 8 in; **avoid cultivation after knee-high**.
- **Mexican Bean Beetle scouting trigger**: order beneficial wasps when larvae appear.

## Notes for FGW calendar translation

- Translated to Zone 6b WV: drying beans go in **early-to-mid July** (about 2 weeks after Twin Oaks 6/25), pushing the dry-bean finish very close to first frost — variety selection (~80–90 day varieties) is critical at this latitude.
- The "avoid cultivation after knee-high" rule maps cleanly to FGW's "no more disturbance once the crop is up" prescription.
- MBB scouting trigger is a chapter-side addendum FGW posters don't carry.
