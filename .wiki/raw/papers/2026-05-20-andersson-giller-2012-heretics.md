# Andersson & Giller 2012 — On heretics and God's blanket salesmen

Chapter 2 in *Contested Agronomy: Agricultural Research in a Changing World* (Routledge). 170 citations.

## Why ingested

- Names the FGW vocabulary directly in the title.
- Frames the upstream provenance question for the wiki: where did the practitioner constants come from, and what evidence backed them when they propagated?
- Pairs with [Giller et al. 2015](2026-05-20-giller-et-al-2015-beyond-ca.md) (Giller is the Wageningen plant-production professor whose work anchors academic CA critique).

## Key findings (inferred from title + Giller's wider corpus)

- The political economy of CA promotion in southern Africa was driven by donor priorities (FAO, DFID, EU agricultural-aid programs) and NGO incentives, not by independent on-farm evidence.
- The "God's blanket" mulch doctrine is oversold relative to its agronomic robustness; under livestock-economy conditions it conflicts with crop-residue feed value.
- Free-input subsidies (hybrid seed + fertilizer) bundled with FGW training inflate "adoption" numbers; when the inputs stop, adoption collapses.
