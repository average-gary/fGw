# Heritage Beans (WVU Extension, Lewis W. Jett)

## Why ingested

The only authoritative source the search surfaced that names regionally-adapted dry-bean cultivars a WV grower should actually use. FGW's posters specify "beans" generically; the chapter-side companion needs the variety list to be useful.

## Key findings

- **Appalachian-adapted dry / shell pole bean varieties**: Coal Camp, Fat Man, October Tender Hull, Turkey Craw.
- **Half-runner replacements**: Volunteer, Josephine Jackson, Brown-Seed NT.
- **Succession window**: every 4 weeks **May through early July** — the WV calendar anchor for sequenced bean plantings.
- Notes transplanting trials for earlier first-harvest dates; high-tunnel option for season extension at the cool-spring / cool-fall edges.

## Notes for FGW calendar translation

- A FGW pile in WV oriented toward bean rows should anchor on these cultivars; substitution for tropical cowpeas / climbing beans isn't a clean swap.
- 4-week succession is wider than the 2–3-week succession the green-bean bulletin recommends; Jett's "every 4 weeks" reflects pole + dry beans on a longer set-to-pod timeline.
- October Tender Hull has, by name, the latest finish — pushes the Zone 6b window up against first-frost; treat as the conservative last-plant-date variety.
